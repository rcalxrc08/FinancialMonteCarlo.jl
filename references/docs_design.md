# Documentation design

_The site, the manual, the shared design._ (Layer 3 reference.)

- The design is shared by the docs of all the rcalxrc08 packages (identical copies in every repository, edit them
  together): `docs/src/assets/style.css` (Dark Quant palette), `dark.js` (dark theme by default), `figures.css`,
  `site.css` (page headers with kicker, the first paragraph as lede, chips and a strip of section links; numbered
  sections; figure, maths, table, docstring and footer cards) and `home.css` (landing page: hero, stat tiles, feature
  cards, flow diagram, results behind radio-button tabs, quick start steps).
- `index.md` is the landing page; `docs/figures.jl` (run by `make.jl` in a module of its own) simulates its figures at
  build time, a light and a dark SVG each in `docs/src/assets/figures/` (git-ignored): paths, convergence with
  `confinter`, terminal densities, and the hero (`hero_figure`), 160 Heston paths coloured by their final spot.
- The tutorials are generated by Literate from `docs/literate/*.jl` into `docs/src/tutorials` and have no page header.
- The manual separates mechanics from catalogues: `models.md` (how models are built and simulated) and `payoffs.md` (how
  payoffs are evaluated, smiles, positions) against the catalogues `bestiary.md` ("Model bestiary": a card per model,
  its dynamics written for the log return `X_t = log(S_t/S_0)` (Bachelier for `S_t`, prices can be negative),
  constructor, parameters with constraints, scheme, known issues, and a support table: scheme, GPU tested, Enzyme,
  closed form) and `payoff_bestiary.md` (every payoff, what it pays on the grid, its engine, its Black-Scholes closed
  form). A new model gets its card there.
- Keep the docs in step with the code in the same commit: the manual pages in `docs/src/manual/` (their kickers are
  numbered `Manual NN` in the order of `make.jl`), `docs/src/api.md` (every exported or documented name),
  `docs/src/extending.md`, the Literate tutorials, the docstrings. Measured numbers in the docs (performance and
  autodiff tables) say on which machine they were taken; refresh them when a change moves them.
- Closed forms in the docs come from FinancialToolbox, never written by hand.
- The logo and favicon live in `etc/` (drawn by `etc/logo.jl`).
